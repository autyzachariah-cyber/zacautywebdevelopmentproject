Chisholm Institute is one of Victoria's leading government providers of vocational training and higher education. We proudly serve the diverse communities of Melbourne's south east, with seven campuses located in Berwick, Cranbourne, Dandenong, Frankston, Rosebud, Springvale and Wonthaggi. From short courses to degrees and postgraduate studies, our courses are delivered on campus, online, in the workplace, and offshore. Our community extends to over 100,000 Chisholm graduates and more than 1,500 current staff. We take pride in our diverse student body and workforce and welcome differences in gender, sexual orientation, age, ethnicity, cultural background, religion, and ability.
